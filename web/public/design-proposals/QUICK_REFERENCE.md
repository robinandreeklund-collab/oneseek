## 🔧 Customization Quick Tips

### Change Primary Color

**Design 6 (Compact Pro):**
```css
/* Find and replace #79c0ff with your color */
--primary: #your-color;
```

**Design 7 (Gradient Modern):**
```css
/* Find and replace in gradient backgrounds */
gradient(135deg, #667eea 0%, #764ba2 100%)
```

**Design 8 (Card Based):**
```css
/* Find and replace #667eea with your color */
--primary: #your-color;
```

**Design 9 (Terminal Style):**
```css
/* Find and replace #79c0ff with your color */
--accent: #your-color;
```

**Design 10 (Floating UI):**
```css
/* Find and replace #00d4ff with your color */
--neon: #your-color;
```
